Cleaning Services in Dar es Salaam

AJPLUS Cleaning Services is part of AJ PLUS COMPANY LIMITED, the same company behind AJPLUS Security Services. We provide professional cleaning for offices, commercial buildings, industrial sites and newly completed construction projects in Dar es Salaam, with the full range of services also available through our Mafinga and Babati branches.

Whether you need a one-off deep clean or a regular cleaning programme for your premises, tell us about your site and we will propose a plan that fits it.

What's Included

Deep Cleaning

Thorough top-to-bottom cleaning of floors, surfaces, washrooms and hard-to-reach areas.

Office Cleaning

Regular cleaning for offices and commercial spaces, so your team and visitors always find a clean workplace.

Industrial Cleaning

Cleaning for factories, warehouses and industrial premises, planned around your operations.

Post-Construction Cleaning

Removal of dust, debris and residue after building or renovation work, ready for handover.

Who We Clean For

  • Companies and organisations that need clean, well-kept offices and commercial spaces.
  • Factories, warehouses and other industrial sites.
  • Developers, contractors and property owners preparing a completed building for handover.
