Mobile Patrol & Alarm Response

Not every site needs a guard standing at the gate all day — many need confidence that someone is checking, often, and unpredictably. AJ Plus Mobile Patrol & Alarm Response runs scheduled and randomized vehicle patrols across your sites, with rapid-response teams dispatched once an alarm has been verified.

Every alarm is verified before a response team is dispatched, reducing false-alarm callouts and keeping our teams focused on real incidents.

Alarm Received → Verification → Dispatch → Response → Incident Assessment → Client Notification → Report

What's Included

Randomized Patrol Routes

Unpredictable patrol timing across your sites makes it harder for anyone to plan around our presence.

Alarm-Triggered Dispatch

The moment an alarm fires, the nearest patrol vehicle is dispatched directly to your location.

Fast Response Times

Our average response time keeps incidents from escalating before a team arrives on scene.

Who Relies On Mobile Patrol & Alarm Response

  • Retail chains and branch networks that need consistent checks across many locations.
  • Residential estates that want regular patrol passes without a full-time static guard.
  • Businesses with existing alarm systems that need a real team behind every alert.
