Privacy Policy

Who we are

This website is operated by AJ PLUS COMPANY LIMITED, trading as AJPLUS Security Services, based in Salasala, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. This policy explains what personal information we receive through this website and how we handle it. Last updated: November 2026.

Information we receive

We only receive the information you choose to give us:

  • Quote and contact requests: when you use the quote form, you enter details such as your name, company, phone number, location, the service you need and a message. The form opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled, and we receive it only when you send it.
  • Calls, WhatsApp and email: any details you share when you contact us directly on +255 762 307 647 or info@ajpluscompany.co.tz.
  • Website usage: we may use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the website. These tools may use cookies and collect technical information such as pages visited and device type.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation.
  • To provide and manage the services you request.
  • To improve this website and our services.

Security service records

Information handled while delivering our security services, such as CCTV footage, guard and incident records, is governed by our agreements with each client. It is not collected through this website.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We rely on trusted providers to run this website and communicate with you, including web hosting, database and messaging services (such as Vercel, Supabase and WhatsApp) and analytics services. They process information only as needed to provide their service. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Keeping and protecting information

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond and to manage the resulting business relationship, and we take reasonable steps to protect information from loss or unauthorised access.

Your rights

Subject to applicable Tanzanian data protection law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.

Contact us

For any privacy question, write to info@ajpluscompany.co.tz or call +255 762 307 647.
